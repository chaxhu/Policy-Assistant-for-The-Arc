# Lost, stolen and damaged cards

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains what to do if a card is lost, stolen or damaged, how quickly we block it, who pays for transactions made with a missing card, and how long a replacement takes.

## How to report a lost or stolen card

Report a lost or stolen card as soon as you notice it is missing. There are two ways to report:

- **Customer portal**: go to **Cards**, select the card and choose **Report lost or stolen**. This is available 24 hours a day.
- **Phone**: call the 24 hour card line on 01632 960 123 (a fictional number for this demo).

Drivers can report a card themselves by phone. Only fleet managers and administrators can report through the portal. We recommend that drivers also tell their fleet manager straight away.

If the card was stolen, report the theft to the police and ask for a crime reference number.

## How fast the card is blocked

A card reported through the portal is blocked immediately. The block reaches every site in our network within 5 minutes. A card reported by phone is blocked within 15 minutes of the end of the call.

A blocked card cannot be unblocked. If a card you reported turns up later, do not use it. Cut through the chip and magnetic strip and dispose of it safely.

## Liability before and after reporting

The customer is liable for all transactions made with the card before it is reported lost or stolen.

After the card has been reported, Sample Fuel Card Co. covers any transactions made with it, as long as the customer has met the security rules in the PINs, security and fraud policy. For example, we will not cover transactions if the PIN was written on the card or kept with it.

If a stolen card was used before it was reported, we can review those transactions. To ask for a review, send us the crime reference number within 7 days of reporting the card. A review does not guarantee a refund.

## Replacement cards

Replacement cards are ordered automatically when a lost or stolen card is reported through the portal. If you report by phone, the agent will order the replacement during the call.

- **Standard replacement**: free, delivered within 3 working days.
- **Express replacement**: delivered the next working day if ordered before 2pm. There is a £10 fee for express delivery.

Replacement cards have a new card number and a new PIN. The PIN is posted separately and arrives within 2 working days after the card. Product restrictions and controls are copied from the old card.

## Damaged cards

If a card is damaged, worn or the chip stops working, order a replacement in the portal under **Cards**, then **Replace damaged card**. Damaged card replacements are free and arrive within 5 working days. The damaged card keeps working until the new card is activated, so drivers are not left without a card.

When the new card arrives, cut through the chip of the old card and dispose of it.

## Checklist for drivers

1. Check the vehicle and your belongings.
2. Report the card by phone or tell your fleet manager.
3. If stolen, get a crime reference number from the police.
4. Pay for fuel another way until the replacement arrives, and keep the receipts.
