# Card usage and product restrictions

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains what drivers can buy with a Sample Fuel Card Co. card and how fleet managers control this for each card. It applies to every card on the account from 1 January 2026 and replaces version 1.0 of this policy.

## Fuel types allowed

Cards can be used to buy unleaded petrol, diesel and LPG at any site in the Sample Fuel Card Co. network. Premium fuels, such as super unleaded and premium diesel, are only allowed on cards where the fleet manager has switched on the "Premium fuel allowed" setting. This setting is off by default for new cards.

Red diesel (gas oil) and heating oil cannot be bought with the card. Fuel must only be put into the vehicle linked to the card, or into an approved fuel container for a company vehicle or machine.

## AdBlue

AdBlue can be bought with the card. This covers AdBlue from the pump and packaged AdBlue up to 10 litres per transaction. AdBlue is included in the "Fuel, oil and services" profile and can be added to a custom profile.

## Engine oil, lubricants and screenwash

Engine oil, lubricants and screenwash can be bought on cards with the "Fuel and oil" profile or higher. Each transaction for these items is limited to £40.

## Car wash

Car washes are allowed on cards with the "Fuel, oil and services" profile. Each car wash is limited to £20, and a card can be used for a maximum of 4 car washes per calendar month.

## Shop items and cash

Shop items are not allowed on any card. This includes food, drinks, tobacco, lottery tickets, gift cards and phone top ups. Cash withdrawals and cashback are never allowed, whatever profile is set on the card. If a driver tries to buy a blocked item, the transaction is declined at the till and the driver will need to pay another way.

## Product restriction profiles

Every card has one product restriction profile. The standard profiles are:

- **Fuel only**: road fuel only.
- **Fuel and oil**: road fuel, engine oil, lubricants and screenwash.
- **Fuel, oil and services**: everything in "Fuel and oil", plus AdBlue and car washes.
- **Custom**: the fleet manager picks individual product groups.

New cards are issued with the "Fuel only" profile unless the fleet manager chooses another profile when ordering.

## How fleet managers set restrictions

Fleet managers can change the profile for a card in the customer portal. Go to **Cards**, select the card, then open **Restrictions**. You can change one card at a time or select several cards and apply the same profile to all of them.

Changes to restrictions take effect within 2 hours. Until the change is live, the previous profile still applies. The portal shows a "Pending" label next to any card with a change that is not yet live.

Only users with the Fleet Manager or Administrator role can change restrictions. Drivers cannot change the restrictions on their own card.

## Mileage and vehicle prompts

Fleet managers can switch on an odometer prompt for each card. When it is on, the driver must enter the vehicle mileage at the till before the transaction is approved. Mileage figures appear on invoices and in portal reports.
