# EV charging and mixed fleets

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how to use a Sample Fuel Card Co. card to pay for public electric vehicle charging, which charging networks are included, how charging appears on invoices and how to set up cards for a fleet with both fuel and electric vehicles.

## Using the card for public charging

A card can be used for public charging if the "EV charging" product group is switched on. Drivers can start a charging session in three ways:

- tap the charging tag (RFID key fob) that we send with each EV enabled card;
- use the Sample Charge app, linked to the card;
- tap the fuel card on the contactless reader, where the charger has one.

A PIN is not needed for charging sessions started with the tag or the app.

## Networks included in this sample

Charging is accepted on the following networks. These network names are fictional and used only for this demo:

- **VoltLine** (fictional): rapid chargers at motorway services.
- **Roadside Rapid** (fictional): rapid and ultra rapid chargers on A roads.
- **CityCharge Hubs** (fictional): fast chargers in town centre car parks.
- **Northern Grid Points** (fictional): fast and rapid chargers in the north of England and Scotland.

Charging on any other network is not covered and the session will be declined. The list of networks is updated in the portal under **Help**, then **EV networks**.

## How charging appears on invoices

Charging sessions appear on the same invoice as fuel, as separate lines marked "EV". Each line shows the network, the charger location, the session start and end time, the energy delivered in kWh, the price per kWh and the VAT.

The price per kWh is the price set by the network operator at the time of the session. Overstay or idle fees charged by a network are passed on to you and shown as a separate line.

## Home charging is not covered

The card cannot be used to pay for charging at a driver's home, and we cannot refund home charging costs. If you pay drivers back for charging at home, you will need to do this through your own expenses process.

## Setting up cards for mixed fleets

For a fleet with both fuel and electric vehicles, we suggest one card per vehicle with the right profile:

- **Fuel vehicles**: any fuel profile, with EV charging switched off.
- **Plug-in hybrids**: a custom profile with fuel and EV charging both switched on.
- **Fully electric vehicles**: the "EV only" profile, which blocks all liquid fuel.

The "EV only" profile helps stop fuel being bought on a card linked to an electric car.

## Reporting

The portal's **Reports** area shows charging sessions, total kWh and charging spend for each card, vehicle and cost centre. You can download these reports as CSV files.
