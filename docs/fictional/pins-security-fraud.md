# PINs, security and fraud

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains the PIN rules for our cards, the alerts and controls that help stop misuse, and what happens when we suspect fraud.

## PIN rules

Every card has a 4 digit PIN. The PIN is posted separately from the card and arrives within 2 working days after it. A PIN must be entered for every transaction.

The PIN is locked after 3 incorrect attempts. A fleet manager can unlock it in the portal under **Cards**, then **Unlock PIN**, or by calling the 24 hour card line. Unlocking takes effect within 10 minutes.

If a driver forgets the PIN, the fleet manager can request a PIN reminder in the portal. PIN reminders are only sent by post to the account address. We never give out PINs by phone, email or text message.

Drivers must never write the PIN on the card or keep it with the card. Sample Fuel Card Co. will never ask anyone for a PIN.

## Suspicious transaction alerts

Fleet managers can receive alerts by email and text message when a transaction looks unusual. Alerts are sent for:

- a fuel volume larger than the tank capacity recorded for the vehicle;
- more than 3 transactions on one card in 24 hours (you can change this number);
- any transaction outside the UK;
- transactions between 11pm and 5am, if the night time control is switched on.

Alerts are switched on for all new accounts. You can choose who receives them under **Settings**, then **Alerts**.

## Velocity and location controls

Fleet managers can add controls to each card to reduce the risk of misuse:

- **Velocity controls** limit the number of transactions per day and the amount spent per transaction or per day. A transaction over a limit is declined at the till.
- **Location controls** restrict a card to chosen regions of the UK or to a list of named sites.
- **Time controls** restrict a card to chosen days of the week and hours of the day.

Controls are set in the portal under **Cards**, then **Controls**, and take effect within 2 hours.

## When fraud is suspected

If our monitoring system or an alert suggests fraud, we may block the card straight away to protect the account. We will then contact the fleet manager within 1 working hour during business hours (8am to 6pm, Monday to Friday). Outside business hours we will contact you at the start of the next business day.

The card stays blocked until the fleet manager confirms whether the transactions were genuine. If they were genuine, we remove the block. If fraud is confirmed, the card is cancelled and a replacement is sent free of charge.

We aim to complete a fraud investigation within 10 working days. We will refund confirmed fraudulent transactions where the customer has kept to the security rules in this policy.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep cards and PINs separate and secure.
- Remove cards from vehicles that are left unattended overnight where possible.
- Check transactions in the portal at least once a week.
- Cancel cards for drivers who leave your business on their last day.
- Tell us straight away about any transaction you do not recognise.
