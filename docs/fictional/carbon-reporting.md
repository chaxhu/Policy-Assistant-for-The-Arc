# Carbon reporting and offsetting

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains the emissions reports we provide, how we estimate emissions from the fuel and electricity bought on your cards, and how our optional offsetting scheme works.

## Emissions reports available

All customers can use the following reports at no extra cost, in the portal under **Reports**, then **Carbon**:

- **Monthly emissions report**: estimated emissions for each card, vehicle and cost centre for the month.
- **Annual emissions summary**: totals for the year, split by fuel type, with a comparison to the previous year.
- **Fuel efficiency report**: litres or kWh per 100 miles for each vehicle, where odometer readings have been entered.

Reports can be downloaded as PDF or CSV. Monthly reports are ready by the 5th working day of the following month.

## How CO2e is estimated

We estimate emissions in kilograms of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e). For liquid fuels, we multiply the litres bought by a conversion factor for that fuel type. For electric charging, we multiply the kWh delivered by a factor for UK grid electricity.

We use the UK Government greenhouse gas conversion factors for company reporting. We update the factors in our reports each year, within one month of the new factors being published. Reports always show which year's factors were used.

These figures are estimates based on what was bought on the card. They do not measure what came out of the exhaust, and they do not include fuel bought another way or emissions from making the vehicle.

## Optional offsetting scheme

Customers can choose to offset the estimated emissions from their fuel card purchases through our optional offsetting scheme.

- **Price**: £15 per tonne of CO2e, worked out from the monthly emissions report.
- **Billing**: the offset charge is added to the first invoice of the following month as a separate line.
- **Projects**: offsets are bought from independently verified projects. The portal lists the projects used each year.
- **Certificate**: you receive an offset certificate each year showing the tonnes offset.

To join, go to **Reports**, then **Carbon**, then **Offsetting**, and choose **Join scheme**. You can leave at any time and the change applies from the next month.

## What offsetting does not do

Offsetting does not reduce the emissions from your vehicles. We recommend using the emissions and fuel efficiency reports to find ways to cut fuel use first, for example by reviewing routes, driver behaviour and vehicle choice, and then offsetting what is left.
