# Advisory fuel rates

> Summary of public UK guidance retrieved on 2026-10-01. Always check the source for the latest version.

## What advisory fuel rates are for

Advisory fuel rates are published by HMRC for employees who drive a company car. They can only be used in two situations:

- to reimburse employees for business travel in their company car; or
- to work out how much an employee must repay their employer for fuel used on private travel in a company car.

The guidance says you must not use these rates in any other circumstances. The rates only apply to employees using a company car. They are not for employees using their own car.

## Tax and National Insurance effect

If you reimburse business travel at no more than the advisory fuel rates, there is no taxable profit and no Class 1A National Insurance to pay. You can pay a higher rate if you can show that the fuel cost per mile is genuinely higher.

For private travel, if the employee repays the cost of private fuel at the right rate and mileage is recorded correctly, no fuel benefit charge applies.

## Rates for petrol cars from 1 September 2026

| Engine size | Rate per mile |
|---|---|
| 1400cc or less | 14 pence |
| 1401cc to 2000cc | 17 pence |
| Over 2000cc | 27 pence |

## Rates for diesel cars from 1 September 2026

| Engine size | Rate per mile |
|---|---|
| 1600cc or less | 15 pence |
| 1601cc to 2000cc | 16 pence |
| Over 2000cc | 22 pence |

## Rates for LPG cars from 1 September 2026

| Engine size | Rate per mile |
|---|---|
| 1400cc or less | 11 pence |
| 1401cc to 2000cc | 13 pence |
| Over 2000cc | 20 pence |

## Advisory electricity rate for fully electric cars from 1 September 2026

The advisory electricity rate (AER) applies to fully electric company cars. From 1 September 2026 there are two rates, depending on where the car is charged:

- **Home charging**: 7 pence per mile.
- **Public charging**: 15 pence per mile.

For journeys where a company car is charged at both public and home (residential) locations, you can split the mileage based on how much charging happens at each place.

Electricity is not treated as a fuel for car fuel benefit purposes.

## Hybrid cars

Hybrid cars are treated as either petrol or diesel cars for advisory fuel rates. There is no separate hybrid rate.

## When rates change

HMRC reviews the rates every quarter, on 1 March, 1 June, 1 September and 1 December. You can keep using the previous rates for up to 1 month from the date any new rates apply.

The rates are worked out from manufacturer fuel economy (MPG) figures weighted by fleet sales and from government fuel price data. The electricity rates use data from the Department for Transport and the Office for National Statistics.

## Previous rates (1 June 2026 to 31 August 2026)

A few examples of the rates that applied before 1 September 2026, as shown on the page:

- Petrol, over 2000cc: 26 pence per mile.
- Diesel, 1601cc to 2000cc: 17 pence per mile.
- LPG, over 2000cc: 21 pence per mile.

Check the GOV.UK page for the full table of past rates.

## VAT

Businesses that reimburse fuel using these rates may be able to reclaim VAT on the fuel element of the payment. See the separate guidance on VAT on motoring expenses (VAT Notice 700/64), which accepts these published rates for working out the fuel element of a mileage allowance.
