# Invoicing and billing

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains how often we invoice, what our invoices contain, how to get them from the portal, when payment is taken and how to dispute a transaction.

## Invoice frequency

Invoices are issued weekly by default. Each weekly invoice is dated Monday and covers all transactions from the previous Monday to Sunday.

Customers can ask to move to fortnightly invoicing at any time. Monthly invoicing is available on request but needs approval from our accounts team. Changes to invoice frequency start from the next full invoice period.

## VAT invoices

Every invoice is a full VAT invoice. It shows each transaction with the date, site, product, quantity, net price, VAT rate and VAT amount, plus totals for the invoice period. Our VAT registration number is printed on every invoice.

Products bought at different VAT rates are shown on separate lines, so your finance team can see the VAT for each product.

## Downloading invoices from the portal

Invoices are available in the customer portal from 9am on the invoice date. Go to **Billing**, then **Invoices**. You can download each invoice as a PDF, and the transaction lines as a CSV file for your own systems.

The portal keeps invoices for the previous 24 months. For older invoices, contact our accounts team and we will send copies within 5 working days.

Users with the Finance or Administrator role can download invoices. Fleet managers can see transactions but cannot download invoices unless they also have the Finance role.

Paper invoices can be posted on request. There is a charge of £2 for each paper invoice.

## Payment terms

Payment is taken by Direct Debit 14 days after the invoice date. If the 14th day is not a working day, we take payment on the next working day.

If a Direct Debit fails, we will contact you and try again after 3 working days. If payment is still not received 7 days after the original due date, cards on the account may be placed on hold until payment is made.

## Disputing a transaction

If you think a transaction on your invoice is wrong, you can dispute it within 60 days of the invoice date. To raise a dispute:

1. Go to **Billing**, then **Disputes**, in the portal.
2. Select the transaction and choose a reason, such as wrong amount, duplicate charge or not recognised.
3. Add any receipts or notes that help us investigate.

You can also email disputes@samplefuelcard.example with the card number, transaction date, site and amount.

We will acknowledge a dispute within 2 working days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. While a dispute is open, you should pay the rest of the invoice as normal. The disputed amount is not taken until the dispute is resolved. If the dispute is upheld, we will issue a credit note on your next invoice.

If you do not recognise a transaction and think the card may have been used by someone else, also follow the PINs, security and fraud policy.
