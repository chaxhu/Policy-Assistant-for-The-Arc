# Card usage and product restrictions

> Fictional sample policy for a concept demo. Not the terms of any real company.

## Purpose and scope

This policy explains what drivers can buy with a Sample Fuel Card Co. card and how fleet managers control this for each card. It applies to every card on the account from 1 March 2024.

## Fuel types allowed

Cards can be used to buy unleaded petrol, diesel and LPG at any site in the Sample Fuel Card Co. network. Premium fuels, such as super unleaded and premium diesel, are allowed on all cards.

Red diesel (gas oil) and heating oil cannot be bought with the card. Fuel must only be put into the vehicle linked to the card.

## AdBlue

AdBlue cannot be bought with the card. Drivers who need AdBlue should pay for it themselves and claim it back through their employer's expenses process.

## Engine oil and lubricants

Engine oil and lubricants can be bought on cards with the "Fuel and oil" profile. Each transaction for these items is limited to £25.

## Car wash

Car washes are not allowed on any card.

## Shop items and cash

Shop items are not allowed on any card. This includes food, drinks, tobacco, lottery tickets and gift cards. Cash withdrawals and cashback are never allowed. If a driver tries to buy a blocked item, the transaction is declined at the till.

## Product restriction profiles

Every card has one product restriction profile. The available profiles are:

- **Fuel only**: road fuel only.
- **Fuel and oil**: road fuel, engine oil and lubricants.

New cards are issued with the "Fuel only" profile unless the fleet manager chooses another profile when ordering.

## How fleet managers set restrictions

Fleet managers can change the profile for a card in the customer portal. Go to **Cards**, select the card, then open **Restrictions**. Changes can only be made one card at a time.

Changes to restrictions take effect by the end of the next working day. Until the change is live, the previous profile still applies.

Only users with the Fleet Manager or Administrator role can change restrictions.
